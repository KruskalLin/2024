I am a Ph.D. student at Texas A&M University supervised by [Prof. Shuiwang Ji](https://people.tamu.edu/~sji/) at [DIVE lab](https://github.com/divelab). Before that, I was a research assistant at Nanjing University advised by [Prof. Yang Yu](https://www.wolai.com/eyounx/dtR1MTyRXS5tP5Cex4KtdK). My research interest is in the broad application of **invariant** and **equivariant** geometric graph learning for 3D atomic systems.
